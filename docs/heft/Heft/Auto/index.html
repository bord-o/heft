Search Infrastructure

type choice_kind =
  1. | CTerm of Tactic.goal * Kernel.term
  2. | CTheorem of Tactic.goal * Kernel.thm
  3. | CTactic of Tactic.goal * Tactic.cost * Tactic.tactic
  4. | CUnknown of Tactic.goal
    (*

    Tag describing what was offered at a given Choose point.

    *)
type search_metadata =
  1. | MSubgoal of Tactic.goal
  2. | MChoice of choice_kind
  3. | MResume
    (*

    Classification of an entry in a search frontier, used by with_best_first to order exploration.

    *)
type step_result =
  1. | Cont of (choice_kind * (unit -> step_result)) list
  2. | Need of Tactic.goal * Kernel.thm -> step_result
  3. | Done of Kernel.thm
  4. | Dead
    (*

    Result of one expansion of the proof tree by step.

    *)

module type Frontier = sig ... end

A container of pending search entries. The choice of frontier determines the search order: stack for DFS, queue for BFS, priority queue for best-first.

val step : Tactic.tactic -> Tactic.goal -> step_result

Runs a tactic until it performs its next Subgoal, Choose, Fail, or completes, and packages the continuation.

Formats counts of subgoals, choices, and resumptions in a list of frontier entries.

Automation Tactics

val itauto : Tactic.tactic

Choice over the basic intuitionistic propositional tactics. Wrap with a search combinator to drive automation.

val ctauto : Tactic.tactic

Like itauto, extended with ccontr for classical propositional logic.

val ctauto_dfs : Tactic.tactic

ctauto wrapped with with_dfs.

val auto_dfs : Tactic.tactic

auto wrapped with with_dfs.

val destruct_elim : Tactic.tactic

A variation of destruct which also eliminates the existentials it generates

val simp_all : Tactic.tactic

A combination of simp_asm and simp

val ac_norm : string -> Tactic.tactic

ac_norm op performs normalization using associativity and commutativity theorems with names of "op_comm_left", "op_comm", and "op_assoc".

val cond : Tactic.tactic

Finds COND applications in the goal, chooses one of their conditions via Choose, and delegates to destruct on the chosen condition.
